# Help & support

**Email:** lesepavistespro@gmail.com — we usually answer within 2 working days.

## How do I get my pets back on a new phone?

Install Pavurio, tap **I already have an account** on the first screen, and sign in with the same Apple, Google or email you used before. Your pets and care history come right back. Photos stay on the old phone.

## Is my data backed up?

Yes. While you use Pavurio, your data is backed up automatically to your account. To be able to restore it on another phone, save your account in **Settings → Account** with Apple, Google or email.

## How do I cancel Pavurio Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by your store:

- **iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pavurio.
- **Android:** Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pavurio.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

## I bought Pro but it isn't active

Open the Pro screen and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same store account you bought with.

## Reminders don't show up

Check that notifications are allowed for Pavurio in your phone settings, and that the routine has reminders turned on (Care → the routine).
