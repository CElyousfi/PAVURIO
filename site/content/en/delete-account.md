# Delete your Pavurio account

You can delete your account and every piece of data linked to it at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Pavurio and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm. For your safety you may be asked to sign in again.

This immediately deletes:

- your account and sign-in;
- your cloud backup (pets, care log, routines, visits, weights, records, moments);
- everything stored on that phone.

It cannot be undone.

## Without the app

Send an email to **lesepavistespro@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my Pavurio account"**. Send it from the email address of your account; if you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email, tell us the date you created the account.

We confirm and delete your account and backup within 30 days.

## What is not deleted

- **Purchase records** are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for accounting and tax law.
- **Crash reports** expire on their own within 90 days.
- **Subscriptions are not cancelled** by deleting your account. Cancel them in your App Store or Google Play settings.
