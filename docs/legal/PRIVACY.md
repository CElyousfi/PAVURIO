# Pavurio — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Pavurio keeps everything you enter **on your device**: your pets, care logs, routines, appointments, weights, health records, attachments and photos. We don't run servers that receive this data, we have no accounts, and we use no analytics or advertising SDKs.

**Notifications** are scheduled locally on your phone from your routines and appointments.

**Purchases** (Pavurio Pro) are processed by Apple or Google. To check whether Pro is active we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts — never your pet data. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

**Photos, camera and files** are accessed only when you choose to attach something; the copies live inside the app's private storage.

**Export and deletion:** Settings → Your data lets you export everything (CSV / JSON) or erase it. Deleting the app also deletes the data.
