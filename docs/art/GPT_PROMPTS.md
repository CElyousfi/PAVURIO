# Pavurio — ChatGPT illustration prompts (Nov 2026)

These prompts replace every placeholder still in the app:
- the Moments covers,
- the Adventures art cut from the small concept sheet,
- the small vector drawings that were made in code.

**How to use**
1. Start one ChatGPT chat and paste **Style block** first. Keep the whole set in that chat so the style stays identical.
2. Then paste one prompt at a time. For pieces in the same family (badges, weather), ask for the whole family in one image ("a sheet of 8, evenly spaced on a grid"), then ask for each one separately at full size.
3. Download each PNG and rename it to the **File** name given. Upload them here and I'll trim, convert and wire them in.

Every image is a **transparent-background PNG**, unless a prompt says otherwise.

## Style block (paste first)

> You are illustrating "Pavurio", a cosy pet-care app. Every image in this chat must share one style:
> - flat, chunky 2D cartoon art with soft gradients and glossy highlights (like candy or gummy);
> - rounded shapes, no hard black outlines (use darker tones of each colour for edges);
> - bright cheerful palette: teal #14B8C4, sunny yellow #FFC83D, coral #FF7A6B, pink #FF6B9A, leaf green #58C322, sky blue #1CB0F6, cream #FFF6E6;
> - little yellow "spark" accents (3 short rounded rays, or 4-point sparkles) and small white daisies with green leaves at the base when it fits;
> - centred subject, generous padding, **transparent background**, no text, no letters, no numbers unless asked, no watermark, no frame, no drop shadow under the canvas.
>
> The two mascots, always drawn the same way:
> - **Honey**: a fluffy puppy with long floppy orange ears, a cream face and chest, a big brown nose, rosy cheeks, a happy open mouth, and a teal bandana with a white paw print.
> - **Tabby**: a grey tabby kitten with darker stripes, a white muzzle and chest, pink ears and nose, rosy cheeks, and a red collar with a gold bell.
>
> Reply with the image only.
