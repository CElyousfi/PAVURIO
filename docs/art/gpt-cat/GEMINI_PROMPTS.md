## Shared rules (paste with every clip)

Animate this exact cat from the image. 16:9, 1280×720, ~8–10 seconds. Static locked camera — no zoom, no pan, no cuts. Pure plain white background for the whole video, no floor, no shadows changing, no scenery. Keep the cat exactly the same character, art style, colours, size and position: grey tabby kitten, white muzzle and chest, pink ears, big black eyes, red collar with a gold bell. Paws stay planted on the same ground line. Smooth, cute, gentle cartoon motion. The cat returns to the exact starting pose in the last second. No text, no extra animals, no hands, no new objects, no sparkles, no hearts, no bubbles, no transparent or glowing effects, no morphing.
