## Two animation systems

1. **Short video clips (main pipeline):** Preserve `docs/art/CHATGPT_ART_BRIEF.md` §5. Gemini generates 6–8-second clips from approved single-pose start frames. Claude processes videos via `tools/art/clips.py`; keep camera static, white background, constant character proportions, no camera motion or text. The cat video queue remains unchecked.
2. **Responsive UI motion (no video dependency):** Use React Native Reanimated transforms on the rendered pet container or layered vector rig. These provide instant feedback while videos load and do not create false care events. See `cat-motion-spec.ts` for named choreography and timing.

## Animation behavior contract

| Motion | Trigger | Duration | Choreography | Loop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| idle | home visible, no other action | 2400ms | breathe scaleY 1→1.015→1; head slight offset if layered | yes |
| blink | occasional idle | 130ms | eyes scaleY 1→0.08→1 using eye layer, not whole image | occasional |
| tap | direct pet tap | 620ms | squash → stretch → settle, 0.96→1.035→1 | no |
| celebrate | persisted care event success | 1050ms | crouch → 10px hop → land → settle; light particles | no |
| eat | meal persisted | 1500ms | optional video or head nod, **never** generated by a button press alone | no |
| drink | water event persisted | 1200ms | optional video or head nod | no |
| play | play event persisted | 1350ms | optional video or paw wave | no |
| groom | care persisted | 1300ms | layered paw lift, or generated video | no |
| litter | litter care persisted | 1100ms | quick gentle hop, or generated video | no |
| sleep | nighttime scene chosen | 2600ms | soft loop; do not imply real pet's health | yes |

## Priority and reliability

- Persist data before celebratory visual transitions; do not fake successful log events.
- User gesture beats ambient idle; a confirmed activity beats ambient motion; never queue an unlimited backlog of celebrations.
- Reduce-motion mode shows a static pet and a clear nonanimated completion indicator.
- Pause ambient animation when app is backgrounded, offscreen, or low-power is enabled.
- All motion must degrade gracefully to the approved still master/avatar, and must not alter recorded care state.
- A video clip is not automatically a Live Activity/Widget asset. For native surfaces use small static imagery and native timers or platform-approved transitions.
- For final video, verify seamless idle loop and consistent feet position. Inspect first and last frames.
